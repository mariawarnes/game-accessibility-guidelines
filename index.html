Each guideline names a barrier players face, then the features that remove it. Drawn from the Xbox Accessibility Guidelines; third-person and shooter titles including The Last of Us Part II, God of War Ragnarök, Call of Duty: Black Ops 6/7, Doom Eternal, Halo Infinite, Apex Legends and Far Cry 6; and other genres including Baldur's Gate 3 (RPG), Forza Motorsport (racing), Celeste (platformer), Civilization VI (strategy) and EA Sports FC (sports).

1Vision

1.1Readable text

Barrier
Text is too small to read.
Solutions
  • UI text size slider
  • Separate subtitle size
  • Icon and HUD scale

1.2Contrast

Barrier
Characters and objects blend into the background.
Solutions
  • High contrast mode: mute the world, colour-code allies, enemies, items and hazards
  • Custom colour per category
  • Dark backing behind HUD elements
  • Crosshair/reticle style, size and colour customisation

1.3Colour vision

Barrier
Information relies on colours some players can't tell apart.
Solutions
  • Protanopia, deuteranopia and tritanopia modes
  • Never use colour alone; add shapes or icons

1.4Magnification

Barrier
Fine detail is hard to see.
Solutions
  • Screen magnifier or zoom

1.5Screen reading

Barrier
Blind players can't read menus or on-screen text.
Solutions
  • Screen reader / text-to-speech for all UI
  • Auto-enable when console TTS is on
  • A button that reads out current status

1.6Audio alternatives to visuals

Barrier
Important cues are only shown visually.
Solutions
  • Audio cues for jumps, ledges, pickups and attacks
  • Audio cue glossary
  • Separate volume slider for cues
  • Blind driving assist: audio cues for racing line, track position and lap timing, each toggleable with adjustable pitch and volume (racing games)

1.7Navigation

Barrier
Players can't find where to go.
Solutions
  • Navigation assist: one button points the camera at the objective
  • Scan / ping for items and enemies, with pitch showing height
  • Dedicated navigation cursor with spoken tile/terrain information for non-visual map exploration (turn-based strategy games)

1.8Safe movement

Barrier
Players fall off edges they can't see.
Solutions
  • Ledge guard with sound and vibration warning

1.9Targeting

Barrier
Aiming at unseen targets is impossible.
Solutions
  • Lock-on
  • Auto-target, including off-screen
  • Assist strength slider

1.10Visual puzzles

Barrier
Puzzles depend on sight.
Solutions
  • Skip puzzle option
  • Puzzle aim assist
  • Visual guides, such as a bounce path

1.11In-world text

Barrier
Signs and notes in the world can't be read.
Solutions
  • Prompt to read aloud or translate

1.12Cutscenes

Barrier
Story told through visuals is missed.
Solutions
  • Audio description

2Hearing

2.1Dialogue

Barrier
Spoken dialogue is missed.
Solutions
  • Subtitles for story and for combat / ambient chatter

2.2Non-speech sound

Barrier
Important sounds are missed.
Solutions
  • Captions for non-speech audio
  • Caption colour option
  • Environmental captions for ambient world sounds

2.3Speaker identification

Barrier
It's unclear who is talking.
Solutions
  • Speaker names
  • Unique colour per speaker

2.4Sound direction

Barrier
It's unclear where a sound comes from.
Solutions
  • Direction arrows for speech and sounds

2.5Subtitle presentation

Barrier
Subtitles are hard to read.
Solutions
  • Size options
  • Background opacity
  • Background blur
  • Text colour

2.6Visual threat cues

Barrier
Threats are only signalled by sound.
Solutions
  • Stealth awareness indicators
  • Damage direction indicators
  • Off-screen enemy indicator
  • Pickup notifications

2.7Haptics

Barrier
No alternative to sound feedback.
Solutions
  • Vibration cues for key events
  • Vibration strength slider

2.8Audio mix

Barrier
Dialogue is lost in the mix.
Solutions
  • Separate volume sliders
  • Voice boost
  • Centre-pan dialogue
  • Mono audio
  • Left / right balance
  • Asymmetrical hearing compensation for unequal hearing between ears
  • Reduce/filter sharp, high-pitched sounds (tinnitus-friendly mix)
  • Enhanced headphone mode

3Motor

3.1Remapping

Barrier
Players can't reach or press some buttons.
Solutions
  • Full control remapping
  • One-handed presets
  • Controller orientation
  • Stick swap
  • Shortcut gestures

3.2Holds

Barrier
Holding buttons is painful.
Solutions
  • Toggle option for every hold: aim, block, sprint, crafting

3.3Repeated presses

Barrier
Button mashing is difficult.
Solutions
  • Hold instead of mash

3.4Precision inputs

Barrier
Quick-time events and mini-games need speed and precision.
Solutions
  • Single-button or auto-complete mode
  • Longer timing windows

3.5Camera control

Barrier
Using two sticks at once is hard.
Solutions
  • Camera assist
  • Auto-recentre on attack
  • Strafe assist

3.6Aiming

Barrier
Aiming needs fine control.
Solutions
  • Lock-on and aim assist
  • Slow motion while aiming
  • Gyro aim with tremor filter

3.7Traversal

Barrier
Climbing and jumping need complex inputs.
Solutions
  • Traversal assist: auto climb, vault and jump

3.8Reduced busywork

Barrier
Frequent small actions add fatigue.
Solutions
  • Auto pick-up
  • Auto weapon swap
  • Auto equip
  • Auto sprint
  • Fast menu holds
  • Infinite breath

3.9Input devices

Barrier
Standard controllers don't suit everyone.
Solutions
  • Support adaptive controllers
  • Support multiple input types
  • Voice, face-expression and head-movement control (e.g. Cephable integration)

3.10Vehicle handling

Barrier
Braking, steering and throttle need precise, simultaneous input (racing games).
Solutions
  • Independent assist toggles for braking, steering and throttle
  • Choose any combination, from full assistance to none

4Motion sickness

4.1Camera movement

Barrier
Shake, sway and fast camera movement cause nausea.
Solutions
  • Camera shake slider
  • Sway / head bob slider
  • Camera rotation speed sliders
  • HUD-based motion anchors: horizontal line, dot grid, border or vignette (size, opacity, thickness, colour)

4.2Screen effects

Barrier
Blur, grain and full-screen effects are disorienting.
Solutions
  • Motion blur slider
  • Film grain slider
  • Toggle full-screen status effects

4.3Cinematic camera

Barrier
Some camera shots are disorienting.
Solutions
  • Toggle dolly zoom
  • Cinematic smoothing

4.4Field of view

Barrier
A narrow or close view feels claustrophobic.
Solutions
  • Field of view slider
  • Camera distance slider
  • Persistent centre dot (size and colour)

5Photosensitivity

5.1Flashing

Barrier
Flashing effects can trigger seizures.
Solutions
  • Reduce flashing option
  • HUD flash toggle
  • Test against flash limits (e.g. Harding test)
  • Contrast filter that limits how fast screen brightness can change

6Cognitive

6.1Settings setup

Barrier
Too many settings to work through.
Solutions
  • Presets for vision, hearing, motor and motion
  • Highlight changed settings
  • Reset all

6.2Remembering goals

Barrier
Players forget what to do next.
Solutions
  • Hints on demand
  • Objective reminder
  • Codex of tutorials

6.3Visual clutter

Barrier
A busy screen is overwhelming.
Solutions
  • Toggle each HUD element separately

6.4Time limits

Barrier
Timers create pressure.
Solutions
  • Remove or extend time limits
  • Pause anywhere

6.5Menu clarity

Barrier
Menus are confusing.
Solutions
  • Consistent navigation
  • Clear focus highlight
  • Tooltips

6.6Mistakes

Barrier
Accidental choices can't be reversed.
Solutions
  • Confirm destructive actions
  • Undo

7Difficulty

7.1Overall challenge

Barrier
The game is too hard.
Solutions
  • Multiple difficulty levels
  • Change difficulty any time

7.2Granular tuning

Barrier
Only one kind of challenge is the problem.
Solutions
  • Separate sliders for damage taken, enemy aggression, stealth and resources

7.3Targeted modifiers

Barrier
Specific mechanics block progress.
Solutions
  • Enemies don't flank
  • Invisible while prone
  • Enhanced dodge
  • Easier boss retries
  • Adjustable game speed
  • Optional invincibility or infinite stamina
  • Skip a level or chapter entirely
  • Adaptive dice/RNG assistance that improves odds after repeated failures

7.4Forgiveness

Barrier
Failure is punishing.
Solutions
  • More checkpoints
  • No achievements locked behind difficulty

8Social and communication

8.1Voice chat

Barrier
Some players can't hear or speak in voice chat.
Solutions
  • Speech-to-text and text-to-speech chat
  • Ping systems
  • Quick chat

8.2Safety

Barrier
Toxic players harm the experience.
Solutions
  • Mute, block and report

8.3Wellbeing

Barrier
Content or long sessions affect mental health.
Solutions
  • Content warnings
  • Break reminders
  • Toggle the appearance of phobia-triggering enemies (e.g. spiders)

9Before and around the game

9.1First launch

Barrier
Players can't find settings before play starts.
Solutions
  • Show accessibility options before gameplay
  • Auto-detect console settings like TTS and mono audio

9.2Documentation

Barrier
Players can't tell if a game is playable before buying.
Solutions
  • Publish a full accessibility feature list

9.3Support

Barrier
Help channels aren't accessible.
Solutions
  • Offer accessible support channels
