How scoring works

  • Every status comes from a linked source. Nothing is estimated.
  • Met = 1 point. Partial = ½ point. Missing and Unknown = 0 points.
  • Unknown means no source was found. It does not mean the feature is missing.
  • The score is a verified minimum. Games with lots of unknowns may score higher once checked.
  • Coverage shows how much of the checklist has evidence. Under 25% is flagged.
  • Platform shows where a game is available. Accessibility features aren't guaranteed to match across platforms — check the evidence column for platform-specific notes (e.g. "PC only").
  • Sources were checked on 29 September 2026. Older sources are dated so you can judge them.
